## 1. Abstract

- 目标：研究VLA如何从实际经验中提升
- 方法：RECAP（RL with Experience and Corrections via Advantage-conditioned Policies）
	- pre-training a generalist VLA via offline RL: $\pi_{0.6}^{*}$
	- specialized to attain high performance on downstream tasks through on-robot data collection
- 数据：heterogeneous data
	- demonstrations
	- data from on-policy collection
	- expert tele-operated interventions
- 效果：
	- 叠衣服
	- 组装盒子
	- 煮咖啡


## 2. Introduction
**「Practice makes perfect」**VLA也需要不断地练习从而成为某一个方面的大师——强化学习！

> **挑战：**
> 1. design scalable and stable RL methods for large models
> 2. handling heterogeneous data from ==different policies== 
> 3. ambiguous or stochastic reward feedback in the real world
{: .prompt-warning}
   

> **解决方案：RECAP** \
>  enables VLA models to incorporate reward feedback in all stages of the training pipeline, from **pre-training** all the way to **training on data from autonomous execution.**
{: .prompt-tip }



> **贡献：** \
>  for the first time, that a **general-purpose reinforcement learning** recipe with **human reward feedback** and **interventions** can significantly improve both the robustness and throughput of VLA models with experience collected through deployment.
{:.prompt-info}
