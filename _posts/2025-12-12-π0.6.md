## 1. Abstract

- 目标：研究VLA如何从实际经验中提升
- 方法：RECAP（RL with Experience and Corrections via Advantage-conditioned Policies）
	- pre-training a generalist VLA via offline RL: $\pi_{0.6}^{*}$
	- specialized to attain high performance on downstream tasks through on-robot data collection
- 数据：heterogeneous data
	- demonstrations
	- data from on-policy collection
	- expert tele-operated interventions
- 效果：
	- 叠衣服
	- 组装盒子
	- 煮咖啡
